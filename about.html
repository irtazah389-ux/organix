About Organix Global Export

Built to put Pakistani makers directly in front of the world.

Our mission

Pakistan produces some of the world's finest textiles, hand-carved goods, leatherwork, and spices — but small and mid-sized manufacturers and artisans rarely get direct access to international buyers. Organix Global Export removes the middlemen and lets vendors sell straight to buyers abroad, keeping more of every sale.

Why Pakistan

From the textile mills of Faisalabad to the artisan workshops of Hyderabad and Multan, Pakistan's export sector is deep, skilled, and underrepresented online. We built a platform specifically for how these businesses operate — flexible MOQs, direct vendor-buyer communication, and no upfront listing costs.

How we work

We use a commission-only model, similar to Airbnb: vendors list for free, and we only earn when a sale actually completes. Payments are held until the buyer confirms receipt, protecting both sides of every transaction.
