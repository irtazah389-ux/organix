How commission works

There's no upfront cost to list a product. When an order is completed and the buyer confirms receipt, the commission for that product's category is deducted automatically — the rest is released to your payout account.

Example: a $2,250 order in Textiles & Fabric (13% commission) results in a $292.50 platform fee and a $1,957.50 payout to the vendor.
